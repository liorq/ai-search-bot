# SASS SRQ — Services Missing a Price: Sarasota Rates and What Acuity Already Answers

Source page: https://www.sass-srq.com/services/ ("Browse Every Treatment" list, checked 2026-09-20).

The treatment list holds **52 services**; **21 already show a price** and **31 do not**.
For each unpriced service below: the Sarasota going rate (low–high seen on local menus)
and a suggested price that sits where SASS SRQ already prices itself — mid-to-upper
independent studio, above the budget wax chains, below the medspas.

All competitor figures were read off published Sarasota price menus (listed at the bottom).

**Cross-checked against the studio's own booking calendar.** The public Acuity /
Squarespace Scheduling calendar (owner 36374233) carries **59 bookable services and 50
add-ons, every one of them priced**. Where a price already exists there, it replaces the
market-derived suggestion — it is what the client actually pays today. That check changed
three things in this document:

- **Waxing needs no suggestion at all.** Both unpriced waxing pages are fully priced in
  Acuity, 37 line items between them. The suggestions in the Waxing section below were
  superseded; the real menu is in that section now.
- **Five services can be priced by copy-paste** and twelve more map onto an existing
  priced treatment. See "What Acuity already answers" below.
- **Fourteen unpriced services do not exist in Acuity at all** — they have pages on the
  site but nothing bookable behind them. That is a product decision, not a pricing one.

## Four existing prices worth revisiting

1. **Teen Facial $155 is more expensive than the Signature Facial $145.** In Sarasota a
   teen/youth facial runs $95. It should be the cheapest facial on the menu, not the
   most expensive — $95–110 fits.
2. **Back Facial $150.** Sarasota competitors charge $60–65 for a back facial. $150 is
   more than double the local rate; $75–85 is defensible as a premium version. Acuity
   already carries an "Express Back Facial — 35 Minutes" at $80 that the site never shows,
   which is close to the local rate and makes a natural entry tier.
3. **Chemical Peel $50.** That is add-on pricing — Acuity confirms it exactly: the $50 is
   the "Chemical Peel Face" add-on, and there is no stand-alone peel service. A stand-alone
   peel in Sarasota is $90–200. Either label the $50 clearly as an add-on, or open and price
   a stand-alone peel at $95–125.
4. **Lash Lift $72.** Sarasota charges $110–156. Not a bug, but money left on the
   table — $89–95 still reads as fair value locally. The same applies to the Acuity bundle
   "Lash Lift & Tint" at $94 against a local $140–156. Changing either means updating
   Acuity, not just the site.

## Note on the homepage FAQ

The homepage FAQ answers "How much does a facial cost at SASS SRQ?" with "call me for a
price." With 21 prices already published on the services page, that answer costs
visibility — price questions are exactly what AI search engines and Google quote. Replace
it with a real range ("facials run $75–275 depending on the treatment") and keep the
consultation offer after it.
